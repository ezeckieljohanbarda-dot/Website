Former Teams (Amateur to Professional)

  • Finesse Solid (2018) – One of his earliest recorded competitive teams.
  • SGD Omega (2018–2019) – Continued his competitive career in the MLBB scene.
  • Bren Esports / AP.Bren (2020–2021) – Won MPL Philippines Season 6, the M2 World Championship, and the M2 Finals MVP award.
  • ECHO (2021–2024) – Won the M4 World Championship in 2023 and MPL Philippines Season 11.
  • Team Liquid PH (2024–present) – Won MPL Philippines Seasons 13, 15, and 16, as well as the 2025 Mid Season Cup.
